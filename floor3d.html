Why this exists
  1. GPS stops at the door. Smoke, stairwells and steel kill the compass too (ours was up to 90° out in this corridor).
  2. A crew surveys the building once, on a quiet day: floor plan plus photos at each junction. That is the pack shown here.
  3. On the call, a responder takes one photo through the glasses. It is matched on the phone, offline, against the pack: floor, position, facing.
  4. The glasses show an arrow and distance to the target room, the nearest stair, or the way out. Command sees the same dot.
Prototype. Positions are the surveyed photo spots (about 2 m), headings about 20°. Tap any coloured wedge to see what the responder's camera sees from there.
