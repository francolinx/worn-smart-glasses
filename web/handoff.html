  2. A 105 to the 8th-floor elevators. Photo fix in the room, out the north door, photo fix in the corridor, 3 m to Stair E, up seven storeys, then 25 m across floor 8 to the elevator lobby. No elevator at any point.

What is not proven

  • Floors 2 to 8 have plans but no survey photos. Every position there is injected, not measured.
  • Frames marked "at the turn" are simulated positions. The real system only knows where you are when you take a photo.
  • The fire signs do not name rooms, so upper floors show shapes, stairs, elevators and exits only.
  • All photo tests use the survey phone on the survey evening. The glasses camera, smoke and darkness are untested.
  • Storey heights in the 3D view are assumed. Corridor metres do not include the stair climb.
  • Unit names and incidents in the stories are invented for the demo.
