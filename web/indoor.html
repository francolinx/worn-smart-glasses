1 · Point and shoot

Aim at something with detail (signs, doors, posters, furniture), not a blank wall. The photo is matched on this phone against the surveyed photos. No GPS, no compass, no upload.

2 · Go

Survey: do this once per building, before you need it

Each reference: shoot, tap where you stood, then tap a point in the direction you faced. Stand at doorways, corners and junctions; take 3 or 4 directions per spot. More photos means better matches.
